# Family game scoring and judging

This document is the product rulebook for score awards, private answers, and host corrections.

## Family Game Night

- **Act It** and **Don't Say It:** a correct answer earns 100 points. A pass, timeout, skipped card, or forbidden word earns 0.
- **Draw It:** in Couch or Team Play, a correct team answer earns 100 points. In Hosted Play, a correct guesser earns 100; the drawer earns 50 when at least half of the eligible guessers are correct.
- **Guess It:** the clue value falls from 100 to 75, 50, and 25 as clues are revealed. The value shown on the judging button is the value awarded.
- Team games credit the active/guessing team. Individual Hosted games credit the named player.

The room creator and a paired Hosted controller may always see the private answer. In Team Play, the waiting team is the judge for Guess It: its paired controller sees the answer and awards the guessing team's result. In Couch Play, the shared controller deliberately selects **Reveal answer to judge** before the Guess It answer appears. The public display and active guessing team never receive the private answer.

## Family Bible Bee

- Little Learners and Family: 100 points for correct answers.
- Challenge: 125 points; Prep: 150; Hard: 175; Expert: 225.
- Up-Ramp rounds progress through 100, 140, and 180 points.
- Little Learners awards 25 participation points for an attempted answer. Oral “almost” judgments earn half credit, with a 25-point minimum.
- Hosted individual-phone play awards ranked speed bonuses of 50, 40, 30, and so on (minimum 10) to correct players. Couch and Team Play do not use device-speed bonuses because a shared or rotating controller is not a fair speed race.
- A skipped or missed question earns 0. The interface labels this consequence before the host taps it.

## Host discretion

The host can add or subtract 25 or 50 points at any time after play begins, including after the final round. Team games adjust the team; individual games adjust a player. Scores never fall below zero. Every adjustment is listed in visible recent history and the latest adjustment can be undone.

Use adjustments for generosity, a missed tap, or an agreed house rule. They do not change the official round rules above. Starting a replay clears both scores and adjustment history.
