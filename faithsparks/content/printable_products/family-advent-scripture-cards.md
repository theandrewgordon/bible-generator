# Family Advent Scripture Cards — Content

**Faith Sparks · faithsparksprintables.com**

25 days through the Christmas story, from God's first promise to the manger. Designed for families with kids ~4–12, about 10 minutes a day.

**Scripture translation:** World English Bible (WEB), public domain. Every verse below was checked word-for-word against the WEB text (bible-api.com, `translation=web`) on Nov 6, 2026. Partial verses are noted as excerpts. On the cards, nested quotation marks are converted to single quotes for readability; the wording is unchanged.

**Story arc:** Days 1–8 Old Testament promises · Days 9–15 John, Mary & Elizabeth · Days 16–19 Joseph, Bethlehem & the birth · Days 20–23 Shepherds & angels · Day 24 Wise men · Day 25 The Greatest Gift

---

## Day 1 — December 1: God's First Promise

**Scripture:** Genesis 3:15 (WEB)

> I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring. He will bruise your head, and you will bruise his heel.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Even when people made a sad choice, God made a promise. Who keeps their promises in our family?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** This is the first hint of a Rescuer in the Bible. Why do you think God planned the rescue right away?
- **Family Prayer:** Dear God, thank You that You had a rescue plan from the very beginning. Help us trust Your promises. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Make a paper chain with 25 links. Tear one off each day as you count down to Jesus' birthday.

## Day 2 — December 2: A Blessing for Every Family

**Scripture:** Genesis 12:3b (WEB) *(second half of verse)*

> All the families of the earth will be blessed through you.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** God said ALL families would be blessed. Can you name some families you love?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Jesus came from Abraham's family. How does Jesus bring blessing to families all over the world?
- **Family Prayer:** Lord, thank You for our family and for families everywhere. Use us to share Your blessing. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Draw your family tree with stick figures, then pray for one other family you know.

## Day 3 — December 3: A Great Light Is Coming

**Scripture:** Isaiah 9:2 (WEB)

> The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light. Those who lived in the land of the shadow of death, on them the light has shined.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** What helps you feel brave in the dark?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Isaiah calls Jesus a 'great light.' What kinds of 'darkness' does Jesus bring light to?
- **Family Prayer:** Jesus, You are our Light. Shine in our hearts and our home this Advent. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Turn off the lights and read today's verse by flashlight or candlelight (with a grown-up).

## Day 4 — December 4: Names for the Promised King

**Scripture:** Isaiah 9:6 (WEB) *(excerpt; "..." marks omitted words)*

> For to us a child is born. To us a son is given... His name will be called Wonderful, Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Which name for Jesus is your favorite? Why?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Pick one of these names. How does it show us who Jesus is?
- **Family Prayer:** Wonderful Counselor and Prince of Peace, thank You for coming to be with us. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Write the five names on slips of paper. Each person picks one and thinks of a way it describes Jesus.

## Day 5 — December 5: Immanuel: God With Us

**Scripture:** Isaiah 7:14 (WEB)

> Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign. Behold, the virgin will conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** 'Immanuel' means 'God with us.' Where is God with you today?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Matthew 1:23 says this promise came true in Jesus. Why does it matter that God came close instead of staying far away?
- **Family Prayer:** Thank You, God, that You are always with us. Help us remember You are near. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Make an 'Immanuel' sign and tape it somewhere everyone will see it (the fridge or front door).

## Day 6 — December 6: Little Town, Big Promise

**Scripture:** Micah 5:2 (WEB) *(excerpt; first part of verse)*

> But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, being small among the clans of Judah, out of you one will come out to me that is to be ruler in Israel.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Bethlehem was a little town. Do you think God can use little things and little people?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Micah wrote this hundreds of years before Jesus was born. What does that tell us about God's plans?
- **Family Prayer:** Lord, thank You that You use small things for big purposes. Use us, too. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Find Bethlehem on a map or globe. Then build a tiny 'town' with blocks.

## Day 7 — December 7: A Shoot From a Stump

**Scripture:** Isaiah 11:1 (WEB)

> A shoot will come out of the stock of Jesse, and a branch out of his roots will bear fruit.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Have you ever seen a little green plant growing from an old stump? What does it look like?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Jesse was King David's father. Why is a tiny shoot from a stump a picture of hope?
- **Family Prayer:** God, You bring new life where things look finished. Grow hope in our hearts. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Go on a nature walk and look for something green or growing in winter.

## Day 8 — December 8: Get Ready!

**Scripture:** Isaiah 40:3 (WEB)

> The voice of one who calls out, “Prepare the way of Yahweh in the wilderness! Make a level highway in the desert for our God.”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** How do we get our house ready when special guests are coming?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** (Yahweh is God's personal name, often written 'the LORD.') How can we get our hearts ready for Jesus this Christmas?
- **Family Prayer:** Lord, prepare our hearts to welcome You. Clear away anything that gets in the way. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Do a 10-minute family 'tidy-up race' to prepare a room, then talk about preparing hearts.

## Day 9 — December 9: A Surprise for Zacharias

**Scripture:** Luke 1:13 (WEB)

> But the angel said to him, “Don’t be afraid, Zacharias, because your request has been heard, and your wife, Elizabeth, will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John.”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Zacharias and Elizabeth prayed for a baby for a long time. What is something you pray for?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Why do you think God sometimes answers prayers after a long wait?
- **Family Prayer:** Father, thank You for hearing every prayer. Help us keep praying and trusting You. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Start a family prayer list and add one request from each person.

## Day 10 — December 10: Gabriel Visits Mary

**Scripture:** Luke 1:30-31 (WEB)

> The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. Behold, you will conceive in your womb, and give birth to a son, and will call his name ‘Jesus.’”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** What would you do if an angel came to visit you?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Angels often say 'Don't be afraid.' What fears can we bring to God?
- **Family Prayer:** God, when we feel afraid, remind us that You are with us. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Make a simple paper angel from a folded triangle and a circle head.

## Day 11 — December 11: Nothing Is Impossible

**Scripture:** Luke 1:37 (WEB)

> For nothing spoken by God is impossible.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** What is the biggest thing you can think of? Is it too big for God?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** When is it hardest to believe that nothing is impossible with God?
- **Family Prayer:** Mighty God, nothing is too hard for You. Help us trust You with big things and small things. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Memorize today's verse together. Say it in a whisper, a loud voice, and a silly voice.

## Day 12 — December 12: Mary Says Yes

**Scripture:** Luke 1:38a (WEB) *(first part of verse)*

> Mary said, “Behold, the servant of the Lord; let it be done to me according to your word.”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Mary said 'yes' to God. How can you say 'yes' to God today?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Mary didn't know everything that would happen. What does it look like to trust God without all the answers?
- **Family Prayer:** Lord, give us willing hearts like Mary to say yes to You. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Do a secret 'yes' act of service for someone in the family today.

## Day 13 — December 13: A Joyful Jump

**Scripture:** Luke 1:41 (WEB)

> When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Baby John jumped for joy! Can you show us your happiest jump?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Why do you think even baby John was excited about Jesus?
- **Family Prayer:** Jesus, fill us with joy because You came. Let our home be full of gladness. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Have a 'joy jump' dance party to a favorite Christmas song.

## Day 14 — December 14: Mary's Song

**Scripture:** Luke 1:46-47 (WEB)

> Mary said, “My soul magnifies the Lord. My spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior.”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Mary sang to God. What song do you like to sing to God?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** 'Magnify' means to make bigger. How can our words and lives make God look big to others?
- **Family Prayer:** Lord, our hearts rejoice in You! Help us praise You with our words and actions. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Look at something through a magnifying glass, then sing a praise song together.

## Day 15 — December 15: Zacharias Praises God

**Scripture:** Luke 1:68 (WEB)

> Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, for he has visited and redeemed his people.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** When baby John was born, his dad praised God. What can you thank God for today?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** 'Redeemed' means bought back or rescued. How is Jesus our Rescuer?
- **Family Prayer:** Blessed be You, Lord, for coming to rescue us. Thank You! Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Each person writes or draws one 'thank You, God' note to put in a jar.

## Day 16 — December 16: Joseph's Dream

**Scripture:** Matthew 1:20 (excerpt) (WEB) *(middle portion of verse; "..." marks omitted words)*

> ...behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, “Joseph, son of David, don’t be afraid to take to yourself Mary, your wife...”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Joseph listened to God. How do we show we are good listeners?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Joseph had a hard choice. How did trusting God help him do the right thing?
- **Family Prayer:** God, help us listen to You and obey, even when it is hard. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Play 'Simon Says' (or 'Joseph Says') and talk about listening well.

## Day 17 — December 17: His Name Is Jesus

**Scripture:** Matthew 1:21 (WEB)

> She shall give birth to a son. You shall call his name Jesus, for it is he who shall save his people from their sins.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Jesus' name means 'The Lord saves.' Do you know what your name means?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Why do you think God chose a name that tells exactly what Jesus came to do?
- **Family Prayer:** Jesus, thank You for coming to save us from our sins. We love Your name. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Write 'JESUS' in big bubble letters and decorate it together.

## Day 18 — December 18: The Long Trip to Bethlehem

**Scripture:** Luke 2:4 (WEB)

> Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to David’s city, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** What would you pack for a long trip?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Remember Micah's promise (Day 6)? How did God use this trip to keep His word?
- **Family Prayer:** Lord, thank You that You are in control of every journey. Guide our steps. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Take a short family 'journey' walk around the house or neighborhood, retelling the trip.

## Day 19 — December 19: No Room in the Inn

**Scripture:** Luke 2:7 (WEB)

> She gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him in bands of cloth, and laid him in a feeding trough, because there was no room for them in the inn.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Baby Jesus slept in a feeding trough for animals! Where do you sleep?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Why do you think the King of everything was born in such a humble place?
- **Family Prayer:** Jesus, there is room for You in our hearts and in our home. Come and stay. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Make a small manger from a box and add a piece of straw or yarn for each kind act this week.

## Day 20 — December 20: Shepherds in the Night

**Scripture:** Luke 2:8-9 (WEB)

> There were shepherds in the same country staying in the field, and keeping watch by night over their flock. Behold, an angel of the Lord stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** What do shepherds do? Can you make a sheep sound?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Shepherds were ordinary workers. Why might God announce the news to them first?
- **Family Prayer:** God, thank You that Your good news is for everyone, even ordinary people like us. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Make cotton-ball sheep and act out the shepherds in the field.

## Day 21 — December 21: Good News of Great Joy

**Scripture:** Luke 2:10-11 (WEB)

> The angel said to them, “Don’t be afraid, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy which will be to all the people. For there is born to you today, in David’s city, a Savior, who is Christ the Lord.”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** What is the best good news you have ever heard?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** The angel said the news is for ALL people. Who could you share it with this week?
- **Family Prayer:** Thank You, God, for the good news of Jesus. Help us share Your joy. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Make a Christmas card for a neighbor or friend that shares the good news.

## Day 22 — December 22: Glory to God!

**Scripture:** Luke 2:13-14 (WEB)

> Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God, and saying, “Glory to God in the highest, on earth peace, good will toward men.”

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** If you were an angel, how would you praise God?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** What does 'peace on earth' look like in our home this week?
- **Family Prayer:** Glory to You, God in the highest! Fill our home with Your peace. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Sing 'Hark! The Herald Angels Sing' or 'Angels We Have Heard on High' together.

## Day 23 — December 23: The Shepherds Tell Everyone

**Scripture:** Luke 2:16-17 (WEB)

> They came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the baby was lying in the feeding trough. When they saw it, they publicized widely the saying which was spoken to them about this child.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** The shepherds hurried to see Jesus! What makes you hurry because you are excited?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** After meeting Jesus, the shepherds couldn't stay quiet. Why do people want to tell others about Jesus?
- **Family Prayer:** Jesus, make us excited to know You and brave to tell others about You. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Call or video chat a grandparent or friend and tell them the Christmas story.

## Day 24 — December 24: Wise Men Follow the Star

**Scripture:** Matthew 2:10-11 (WEB)

> When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy. They came into the house and saw the young child with Mary, his mother, and they fell down and worshiped him. Opening their treasures, they offered to him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** The wise men brought gifts to Jesus. What gift could you give Jesus?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** The wise men came later, when Jesus was a young child. What does it mean to worship Jesus with our 'treasures'?
- **Family Prayer:** Jesus, You are our King. We give You our hearts, our time, and our love. Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Cut out a paper star and hang it in a window on Christmas Eve.

## Day 25 — December 25: The Greatest Gift

**Scripture:** John 3:16 (WEB)

> For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

- **Little Ones (ages ~4–7):** Happy birthday, Jesus! Why is Jesus the best gift ever?
- **Big Kids (ages ~8–12):** Looking back on Advent, which promise or story meant the most to you? Why?
- **Family Prayer:** Thank You, God, for loving us so much that You gave us Jesus. Happy birthday, Jesus! Amen.
- **Optional Activity:** Sing 'Happy Birthday' to Jesus and share a special treat together.
