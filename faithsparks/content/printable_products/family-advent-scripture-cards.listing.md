## Description
Keep Jesus at the center of your December with 25 simple, low-prep Advent Scripture cards your whole family can enjoy together.

These printable Christian Advent cards walk your family through the Christmas story, from God's very first promise in Genesis, through the prophets, Mary, Joseph, the shepherds and wise men, all the way to the manger. Each day takes about 10 minutes, so it fits at breakfast, in the car, or at bedtime.

★ WHAT'S ON EACH CARD
• A Scripture reading (World English Bible, a public-domain translation that's easy for kids to follow)
• A "Little Ones" question for ages ~4–7
• A "Big Kids" question that goes a bit deeper for ages ~8–12
• A short family prayer
• A simple optional activity using things you already have at home

★ WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Cover page and a "How to Use" guide
• 25 daily Advent Scripture cards (Dec 1–25), 4 per page with cut lines
• 3 bonus cards: a title card ("This set belongs to the ___ family"), an "Our Family Advent Memories" card to fill in on Christmas Day, and a blank card for your own verse or tradition
• 9 pages total, US Letter (8.5" × 11"). Cards are about 3.9" × 5" (cut size)

★ PERFECT FOR
• Families with kids of mixed ages
• Homeschool morning time or Bible lessons
• Sunday school, co-ops, and children's ministry
• Tucking into a DIY Advent calendar or a basket by the table

★ INK-FRIENDLY DESIGN
White backgrounds, thin lines, and simple stars and candles. Prints well in color or black and white.
