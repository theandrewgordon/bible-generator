◀ All worlds

World 1 · Beginnings

1-1 · In the Beginning Tend the garden and name the animals (Genesis 1–2) 1-2 · The First Choice Gather good fruit and follow the true path (Genesis 2–3) 1-3 · Two by Two Lead the animal parade to the ark (Genesis 6–7) 1-4 · The Rainbow Promise Brave the wind, light the rainbow, build an altar (Genesis 8–9) 1-5 · The Tower of Babel Climb the imagined scaffold route; hear why God scattered the people (Genesis 11)

World 2 · The Promise Road

2-1 · Beneath a Thousand Stars Move camp by night; count the stars (Genesis 12, 15) 2-2 · The LORD Will Provide Climb Mount Moriah; God provides a ram (Genesis 22) 2-3 · Water at the Well Help Rebekah water ten thirsty camels (Genesis 24) 2-4 · The Dream at Bethel Gather Jacob's things; the ladder dream (Genesis 28) 2-5 · Joseph's Storehouses Store grain, then feed the hungry (Genesis 41) 2-6 · The Road to Forgiveness Through the sandstorm to Joseph, who forgives (Genesis 42–45)

World 3 · Let My People Go

3-1 · Among the Reeds Explore the bank beside Miriam; Moses is spared (Exodus 2) 3-2 · The Burning Bush Climb the ridge; God calls Moses on holy ground (Exodus 3–4) 3-3 · Let My People Go Frogs, hail and darkness: bring the cattle in; follow the light to Goshen (Exodus 7–10) 3-4 · The Passover Get ready in haste; God passes over where He sees the blood (Exodus 12) 3-5 · Through the Sea God parts the sea; help everyone across (Exodus 14–15) 3-6 · Bread in the Wilderness One dune-to-camp loop; God gives daily bread and water (Exodus 16–17) 3-7 · At Mount Sinai Explore below the boundary; hear God’s commandments (Exodus 19–20) 3-8 · A Place to Meet God Bring gifts; raise the gold boards; God's glory fills the tent (Exodus 25–40) 3-9 · The Walls Fall Down Bring Rahab's family home; march together; God brings the walls down (Joshua 6)

World 4 · Courage and Kindness

4-1 · Ruth's Harvest Glean behind the reapers; Boaz is kind; carry the barley home (Ruth 2) 4-2 · Speak, LORD The evening round; walk with Samuel to Eli; God calls (1 Samuel 3) 4-3 · The Shepherd's Hills Sheep, food for the brothers, five smooth stones (1 Samuel 16–17) 4-4 · Mercy in the Cave Hide from Saul's soldiers; David chooses mercy (1 Samuel 24) 4-5 · Abigail's Peace Mission Load the donkeys; clear the way down the hill; peace with David (1 Samuel 25) 4-6 · A House for Worship Climb the building works; seven years later God's glory fills the house (1 Kings 5–8)

World 5 · Faith Far from Home

5-1 · The Last Little Meal A dry-ravine journey; the LORD provides flour and oil and restores the widow’s son (1 Kings 17) 5-2 · The Still Small Voice Care for tired Elijah; wind, earthquake, fire, then a gentle voice (1 Kings 19) 5-3 · Jonah's Second Chance Storm at sea, ride the great fish, Nineveh turns to God (Jonah 1–3) 5-4 · Wounded for Us Isaiah's vision; carry God's message over the rooftops (Isaiah 6, 53) 5-5 · The Fiery Furnace Stand with the three who would not bow; four in the fire (Daniel 3) 5-6 · The Lions' Den Lanterns through the night; God shut the lions' mouths (Daniel 6) 5-7 · For Such a Time Pray with Mordecai, walk with Esther to the king, run the letters (Esther 4–8) 5-8 · Rebuild the Walls Build, keep watch when the trumpet sounds; God gives success (Nehemiah 4–6)

World 6 · Good News in Galilee

6-1 · Shepherds in the Night Sheep into the fold; the angels' news; hurry to Bethlehem (Luke 2) 6-2 · The Night Road to Egypt Pack quietly; lead the donkey past Herod's lanterns (Matthew 2) 6-3 · Leave Your Nets Cross the harbor after Jesus’ miraculous catch; follow Him (Luke 5) 6-4 · Through the Roof Prepare the way up, then open the roof (Mark 2) 6-5 · Enough for Everyone Serve the crowd when Jesus feeds five thousand (John 6) 6-6 · Be Not Afraid Storm at night: tie down the boat; Jesus comes on the water (Matthew 14)

World 7 · The Way to Jerusalem

7-1 · A Neighbour on the Road Help the hurt traveller to the inn (Luke 10, a parable) 7-2 · Zacchaeus Climb the sycamore; Jesus comes to his house (Luke 19) 7-3 · The Upper Room Prepare the Passover; Jesus washes their feet (Luke 22; John 13) 7-4 · The Cross A quiet walk to Gethsemane, and the cross (Matthew 26–27; John 19) 7-5 · He Is Risen! The empty tomb: run and tell the good news (Luke 24)

World 8 · To the Ends of the Earth

8-1 · Good News for Many Nations The Spirit comes; help visitors from every nation to hear (Acts 2) 8-2 · No One Forgotten Serve every household; seven trustworthy men oversee the daily need (Acts 6:1–7) 8-3 · The Desert Road Run to the chariot; Isaiah 53 explained; "See, here is water" (Acts 8) 8-4 · Over the Wall A light on the road; Saul is changed; down the wall in a basket (Acts 9) 8-5 · Knocking at the Gate The church prays; follow the light out of prison; Rhoda forgets the gate (Acts 12) 8-6 · Songs at Midnight Armor on! The earthquake, the open doors, the jailer believes (Acts 16) 8-7 · The Uproar in Ephesus Books to the fire; shouting crowds; keep Paul and his friends safe (Acts 19) 8-8 · Through the Storm Stand firm in the gusts; not one is lost (Acts 27–28) 8-9 · The Tree of Life All things made new; the gate stands open (Revelation 21–22)

Up to 4 players. Pick a world, then a level, with the D-pad (or arrow keys) and A (or Enter). B / Esc goes back; LB / RB change world. In the lobby: press A on a controller (the first one is Player 1), E for the WASD keys, or Enter for the arrow keys.
