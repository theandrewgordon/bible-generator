# Sound credits

- Footsteps, landing, pick up, put down, doors, building knocks, bump, shield click and page turns:
  **RPG Audio by Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Trimmed and levelled for the game.
- Footsteps on grass, sand, stone and wood, and the landing thump: **Impact Sounds by Kenney** (CC0).
- Collect chime, pop, bubble, jump, cheer, gentle "no": **Interface Sounds by Kenney** (CC0).
- Fanfare: **Music Jingles by Kenney** (pizzicato), CC0.
- **Homeward Light**, the level arrangements, updated world fallback arrangements, five environmental beds and bridge/winch/rope creaks: original synthesized material made for Scroll Quest by `tools/compose-storybook.py`. No third-party music samples, recorded instruments or recorded choir are included. See `score-manifest.json` and `../ART-AUDIO.md`.
- Win jingle: original earlier Scroll Quest composition, rendered by `tests/compose.py`.
- Splash and sheep: built into the game (synthesized).

- Distant thunder: original filtered-noise synthesis for Scroll Quest, rendered by `tools/compose-sinai-thunder.py`. No recordings or third-party samples.
