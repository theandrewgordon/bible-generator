Field Guide: How to Play

One adult reads each question aloud. The player answers without looking at the screen. Mark Correct, With help or Needs practice. The answer is shown after marking. A hint-assisted answer counts separately from independent mastery. Hints are optional. Missed facts are tracked for the next games, with no account required.

PDF bank: Questions have IDs matching your existing five-page printable answer key. Fresh questions: New facts are generated and checked automatically; because they are not in the fixed bank, they do not appear in the PDF.

Offline: Download this HTML and open it in a browser. It needs no server, login, API key, or internet. To use it on FaithSparks Lab with installable offline support later, serve it over HTTPS and add a service worker/PWA manifest.
