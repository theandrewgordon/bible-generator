FaithSparks Group Games

Group games for one shared screen.

Pick a room-code game, cast the display to a TV, and let families or teams join from phones.

Scripture quiz Family Bible Bee

Answer Scripture questions on phones while the TV shows each round.

 Party game Act It Out

Act, clue, guess, or draw secret prompts while the host awards cards.
How it works

Create a room, cast the display, play from phones.

  1. Pick a game Bible Bee is quiz-style. Act It Out is party-style.
  2. Open TV display Cast or AirPlay the display page to the room screen.
  3. Players join Phones use the room code; the host runs the game.
  4. Play for points Use individual scores or teams for a prize moment.
