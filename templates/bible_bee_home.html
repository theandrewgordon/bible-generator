How to play

Start a room, answer on phones, reveal together.

  1. Host creates a room Pick Bible version, rounds, difficulty, and a theme.
  2. Players join Everyone enters the room code on their phone.
  3. Answer each round Choose the verse words or reference before reveal.
  4. Score and review Correct answers earn points; missed passages are easy to review.
