How to play

One player performs. Everyone guesses out loud.

  1. Host creates a room Pick a theme, open the TV display, and let players join.
  2. A player gets the card They act, give clues, draw, or follow the TV clues.
  3. The room guesses Players call answers out loud while the answer stays hidden.
  4. Host awards points Tap “Got it right · +100” or “No point / pass,” then deal the next card.
