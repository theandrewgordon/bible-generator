Open Music & chord charts for a song

On Plan Worship, open the song’s ••• menu and choose Music & chord charts. Resources are private to the currently selected church library.

Add the licensing details

Enter the song’s CCLI number and copyright or attribution notice, then choose Save metadata. These details also help the church licensing report stay complete.

Choose what you are saving

Scroll to Add a chart or source. Give the resource a useful title such as “Sunday band chart,” then choose PDF, ChordPro/text, or Source link.

  • Link only: select the provider, paste its HTTPS address, and save. A link by itself does not copy the remote chart.
  • PDF: upload an authorized PDF up to 20 MB. It remains in its original key.
  • ChordPro/text: upload a .cho, .chordpro, .pro, or .txt file, or paste authorized chord text.

Paste a whole chord page when needed

If your permitted source displays a chord chart as webpage text, select and copy the whole visible page, then paste it into Or paste ChordPro / chord text.

Windows / Chromebook: Ctrl + A, Ctrl + C, Ctrl + VMac: ⌘ + A, ⌘ + C, ⌘ + V

Leave Clean pasted webpage text automatically checked. It removes common menus and footers, extracts chart details, and aligns separate chord rows with their lyrics.

Record the original key and source

Select the chart’s actual original key so transposition is reliable. Add capo or arrangement notes when useful, select the provider, paste the source HTTPS link, and add a short license note such as “SongSelect download under church CCLI subscription; internal worship use.”

Confirm permission and save

For uploaded or pasted material, check the permission confirmation and choose Save church resource. A source link alone does not require the upload confirmation.

Open, transpose, or download

The saved resource shows the actions that apply to it:

  • Open / transpose appears for ChordPro or chart text.
  • Download retrieves an uploaded PDF in its original form.
  • Source reopens the official provider page.

Prepare the musician’s chart

On a transposable chart, choose a key from the top bar. Use A− or A+ for text size, switch between one and two columns, then choose Download PDF or Print.

Always verify the new key, slash chords, capo instructions, arrangement, and every chord before rehearsal.

Share the service’s musician packet

After building the service, open Save, share & export and choose Musician packet. The church-private packet lists the songs in service order and links each saved chart, PDF, or official source. Packet links expire automatically.

If a chart does not look right

  • Use a clearly labelled chord or lyrics view rather than a page dominated by navigation and recommendations.
  • Confirm the original key before transposing.
  • For exact publisher formatting, upload the licensed PDF and use Download instead of conversion.
  • For transposition, paste clean chord text or ChordPro and check that chords align with the correct lyrics.
  • Remove a bad resource and save a corrected copy; the original provider file is never edited.
