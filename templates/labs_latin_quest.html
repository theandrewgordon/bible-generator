Small steps count

Teacher & parent notes
  • Uses the 140 approved photographed cards: 120 vocabulary cards and 20 grammar cards. The unresolved card is excluded.
  • New sessions favor cards that need practice. Hinted and revealed answers return for review. Multiple-choice progress is tracked separately from speak/type recall. Longer sessions stop at the number of distinct cards in the chosen mode. Progress stays in this browser, shared by anyone using this browser profile.
  • Answer checking uses listed meanings and a small set of equivalents. It avoids guessing at misspellings. Use “My answer was also correct” to accept another valid equivalent together.
  • AI-generated OpenAI voice clips are saved and reused. Uses restored Classical pronunciation for this CC practice game. Latin pronunciation is a model approximation; a teacher should check unfamiliar forms.
  • The microphone uses your browser’s speech-recognition service, which may send audio to its provider. Faith Sparks receives no voice recordings or learner answers. Voice input works best for English meanings; type Latin forms if recognition struggles.

Voice: AI-generated by OpenAI. Clips are reused from shared storage and saved on your device when supported. If audio is unavailable, use the visible card and answer controls. No automatic device-voice substitution.
