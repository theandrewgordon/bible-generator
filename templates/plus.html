FAQs

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed via Stripe’s customer portal (link in the top navigation once you’re signed in).

Do I lose access if I cancel?

You’ll keep any downloads you’ve already saved. Your monthly generate limit will return to Free.

Can I switch between Family and Classroom?

Yes, you can change plans from the billing portal. Proration is handled by Stripe.
