Common questions

Which window goes on the projector?

Put the Presenter window on the projector or sanctuary display, then choose Fullscreen. The Remote belongs on the worship leader’s phone or tablet. Stage View is optional and is only for the platform team.

Do I have to save the service before going live?

No. You can choose Go live as soon as items are in the service. Use Save service when the plan should be reused or shared with the team.

Why did a YouTube video not autoplay?

Browsers sometimes block autoplay with sound. Tap play once in the presenter or remote, then use the remote’s play, pause, restart, mute, and sound controls. Always test videos on the presentation computer before the service.

How do I share slides or music with the team?

On Plan Worship, open Save, share & export. Use Mobile slide link for a swipeable phone view, Musician packet for charts and sources, or Lyrics PDF for a printable lyric copy. Shared links expire automatically.

What if the remote disconnects?

The presenter keeps running. Use the keyboard: Left/Right changes slides, C clears words, B makes the screen black, and F enters fullscreen. The remote tries to reconnect automatically; the active-session banner on Plan Worship can also reopen it.

How do I fix a bad import or duplicate song?

Do not approve a bad review. Go back and use a cleaner, clearly labelled source, or edit the existing library song. If a matching song is found, Faith Sparks asks before replacing it. Church owners can also use Library tools → Clean duplicate songs.

Who can edit the shared church library?

Owners, editors, and members can add and edit worship material. Viewers are read-only. Destructive library maintenance, such as duplicate cleanup or a library reset, is limited to the church owner.

One good habit: review imported lyrics, chords, slides, videos, and AI suggestions against the authorized source before Sunday. Faith Sparks organizes your material; your church remains responsible for accuracy and licensing.
