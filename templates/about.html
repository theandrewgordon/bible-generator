Hi, I’m Andrew Gordon — a Christian husband, father of four, homeschool dad, and software developer. I love God and I seek to love my neighbors.

I saw my family regularly needing fun, Bible-based ways to practice handwriting and cursive. I couldn’t find exactly what I was looking for, so I built Faith Sparks Printables to create simple, beautiful worksheets that help kids engage Scripture through copywork, reflection, and creativity.

My hope is that these resources serve families, homeschoolers, and churches by making God’s Word more present in everyday learning.
