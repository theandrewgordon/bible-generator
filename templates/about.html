About Faith Sparks

Faith Sparks helps families and small churches live their faith together with less prep, less friction, and more meaningful shared time. Start with a Bible passage and make something useful for the room: a gathering plan, worksheet, worship service, or game.

Who we are

We’re a homeschool family in sunny Florida piecing this together during naptime and late-night planning sessions. Every worksheet we ship is one we use around our own kitchen table, tested by our kids and shared with our co-op friends.

Who we build for first

Faith Sparks is built first for the homeschool parent—often the mom carrying the planning, teaching, and family rhythms all at once. Church and small-group tools share the same low-prep approach, with their own focused starting path.

Why we built this

It began with an easy way to make Scripture copywork. The same need kept appearing in other moments: preparing a house-church gathering, leading worship from a phone, and helping mixed ages play together. Faith Sparks is the shared home for those focused tools.

Built for real rooms

Core tools are dependable and supported. Newer ideas live openly in Labs until they prove they deserve a permanent place. That boundary lets us experiment without making the main experience feel unfinished.
