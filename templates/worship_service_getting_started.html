Worship getting started

Build and run a Sunday service

Put songs, Scripture, announcements, videos, and presentations in order; check the slides; then run the room from a computer and phone.

Open Plan WorshipAll worship help

Add the service items

Use Add beside each library item. Drag the items in This service into the order people will see them. Use Notes… for private planning reminders.

Save when the plan should be reused

Open Save, share & export and choose Save service if the team will return to this plan. Saving is helpful, but it is not required before going live.

Review every slide

Choose Review slides. Check the exact order, lyric breaks, imported slides, videos, and any amber crowded-text warning. Choose Checklist Complete — Build Deck when it is ready.

Put Presenter on the projector

Back on Plan Worship, choose Go live. The Presenter window is the audience screen: move it to the projector or sanctuary display and choose Fullscreen. Keep that tab open for the whole service.

Open the Remote on a phone or tablet

Choose Remote in Presenter or scan the leader QR code. Use Previous/Next, jump to an item, repeat the chorus, control videos, clear words, or make the screen black. The link is temporary and does not require another sign-in.

Use Stage View only when needed

Open Stage View from the remote or scan its QR code. It shows current and upcoming lyrics, the clock, private messages, and a timer for the platform team; it is not the audience display.

Keep the keyboard backup ready

If the phone loses its connection, Presenter continues working.

The remote tries to reconnect automatically. If a window closes, use the active-session banner on Plan Worship to reopen Presenter or Remote.

End the session when the service is over

Choose End session in Presenter or from the active-session banner. For the team, Save, share & export also offers a Mobile slide link, Musician packet, and Lyrics PDF.

Five-minute Sunday check: confirm the projector display, test Remote and Stage View on the church network, play each video with sound, check the first and last slide, and keep the keyboard shortcuts nearby.
