Worship getting started

Build and run a Sunday service

Put songs, Scripture, announcements, videos, and presentations in order; check the slides; then run the room from a computer and phone.

Open Plan WorshipAll worship help

Add the service items

Use Add beside each library item. Drag the items in This service into the order people will see them. Use Notes… for private planning reminders.

Save when the plan should be reused

Open Save, share & export and choose Save service if the team will return to this plan. Saving is helpful, but it is not required before going live.

Review every slide

Choose Review slides. Check the exact order, lyric breaks, imported slides, videos, and any amber crowded-text warning. Choose Checklist Complete — Build Deck when it is ready.

Open Live Setup on your laptop

Back on Plan Worship, choose Go live. If the quick service check finds unreviewed lyrics, crowded slides, or an online video, read the short warning before continuing. Keep Live Setup on your laptop. Choose Open audience window, move that separate Presenter window onto the AirPlay or projector display, then choose Start presenting there.

Pair the Remote on a phone or tablet

Scan the private iPad remote QR on Live Setup, or use Copy remote link. The Remote opens with simple Back and Next controls plus current and next previews. Choose Full controls for song sections and jumps; More controls contains video, stage, and editing tools. It becomes a two-column booth view on a laptop. The temporary link requires no additional sign-in.

Open Stage View on the laptop

Choose Open stage view on Live Setup. Keep this window facing you while the audience window stays on the external display. Stage View shows current and upcoming lyrics, private repeat cues, messages, and timers. Choose Balanced, Larger current, or Larger next; lyrics stay visible when the audience screen is black or its words are cleared.

Keep the keyboard backup ready

Use Open remote here on Live Setup for laptop keyboard controls if the iPad is unavailable. Focus that Remote window before using the shortcuts.

The Remote reconnects automatically. The active-session banner on Plan Worship reopens Live Setup or Remote. The audience receipt indicator confirms browser updates; check the physical display to verify AirPlay.

End the session when the service is over

Choose End this session under the Remote's More controls, or End session in the active-session banner. Save, share & export also offers a Mobile slide link, Musician packet, and Lyrics PDF.

Five-minute Sunday check: confirm the projector display, test Remote and Stage View on the church network, play each video with sound, check the first and last slide, and keep the keyboard shortcuts nearby.
