Before you play

A computer with a keyboard or gamepad is the best starting point. Local family play supports up to four players with compatible controls. Touch controls are available to try on tablets; phone controllers are not enabled in this release.

The first visit downloads the game’s 3D assets. Give it a moment to load, especially on a slower connection.

Your progress

Progress stays in this browser, separately for each Faith Sparks account. Use Backups in the game’s Family settings to move progress to another device or import a compatible Scroll Quest save. It does not sync automatically between devices.
